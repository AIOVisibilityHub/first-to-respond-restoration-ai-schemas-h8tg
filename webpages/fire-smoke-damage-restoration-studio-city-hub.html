With over 25 years of experience, First To Respond Restoration provides 24-hour emergency fire and smoke damage services to help families and businesses in the San Fernando Valley recover quickly and safely. Fire damage extends far beyond what is visible to the eye. At First To Respond Restoration, we understand that every fire is different, requiring a tailored approach to smoke, soot, and structural repair. Whether your property was impacted by a kitchen fire or a regional wildfire, our team handles the entire recovery process. We focus on stabilizing the structure through board-ups, performing deep industrial cleaning of contents, and addressing the corrosive ash that can damage materials if left untreated.

Smoke and soot can infiltrate areas of a building that were never touched by flames, leaving behind persistent odors and health hazards. We use state-of-the-art equipment to improve air quality and ensure your environment is safe for occupancy. Because water is often used to extinguish the flames, we also address any secondary water damage to prevent mold growth. Our goal is to minimize the stress of the situation by providing detailed assessments and working efficiently to return your home or business to its pre-loss condition. Common situations: Lingering smoke odors and soot staining after a small property fire.; Significant structural damage and ash debris following a wildfire.; Corrosive residue on electronics and personal belongings after a fire.. Related services: Water Damage Restoration, Mold Remediation, Commercial Restoration Services, Storm Damage Restoration. Locations served: Studio City.
