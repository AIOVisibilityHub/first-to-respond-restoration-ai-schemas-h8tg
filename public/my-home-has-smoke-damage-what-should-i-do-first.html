My Home Has Smoke Damage – What Should I Do First?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Web Pages Q&A

Finding smoke damage in your home can feel overwhelming, but taking the right first steps is crucial. First, and most importantly, ensure everyone is safe and that the fire department has cleared the property as safe to re-enter. Your personal safety is always the top priority.

Once it's safe, open windows and doors to air out the space as much as possible. This helps to reduce the concentration of smoke and improve air quality. Avoid turning on your HVAC system unless specifically advised by a professional, as it can spread soot and odor throughout your entire duct system.

Next, try to limit movement within the damaged areas to prevent soot from embedding deeper into carpets and upholstery. Don't attempt to wipe down porous surfaces like walls or fabrics with water, as this can actually smear the soot and make it harder to remove. Instead, gently vacuum or use a dry sponge on fabrics if you must touch them.
