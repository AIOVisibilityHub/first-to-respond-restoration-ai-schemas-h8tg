When Your Apartment Catches Fire: Next Steps

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Web Pages Q&A

Experiencing an apartment fire is a shocking event, and knowing the right 'next steps' can significantly impact your recovery. Once everyone is safe and the fire department has secured the scene, resist the urge to immediately re-enter your unit. Even if the fire seemed small, smoke and soot damage can be pervasive and hazardous, not to mention potential structural issues.

Your first call, after ensuring safety, should be to your landlord or property manager. They are critical in coordinating building-wide repairs and communicating with the building's insurance. Simultaneously, contact your renter's insurance company to begin the claims process for your personal belongings.

Take photos and videos of the damage, but only once it’s safe to do so. For effective cleanup, it’s vital to bring in a professional fire damage restoration service. Trying DIY methods for smoke and soot removal can often worsen the situation or expose you to harmful particles.
