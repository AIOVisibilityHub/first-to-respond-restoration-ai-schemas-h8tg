Mistakes People Make When Trying to Clear Smoke Smell

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Web Pages Q&A

When your home smells like smoke after a fire, it’s natural to want to fix it fast. But sometimes, well-intentioned DIY efforts can actually make things worse or just not solve the problem. Here are some common blunders and how to steer clear of them:

  • Just using air fresheners: These only mask the smell temporarily, they don't eliminate the source. You need to remove the microscopic smoke particles.
  • Not ventilating properly: Opening a few windows helps, but a full cross-ventilation system with fans can move a lot more air. Make sure you're getting fresh air flowing through.
  • Ignoring porous materials: Smoke soaks into fabrics, wood, and even painted walls. Just wiping surfaces isn't enough; you need to clean or replace contaminated porous items.
  • Overlooking HVAC systems: Your heating and cooling system can spread smoke particles and odors throughout your entire home. Make sure to change filters and clean ducts.
  • Delaying professional help: The longer smoke residue sits, the harder it is to remove. Acting quickly, especially for fire damage, is crucial. If you're in the San Fernando Valley, call First To Respond Restoration early on.
