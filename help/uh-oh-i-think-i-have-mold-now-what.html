Uh Oh, I Think I Have Mold – Now What?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Web Pages Q&A

Finding mold in your home can definitely be alarming, and your instincts are right – it’s not something you should just ignore. The 'uh oh' moment is totally valid because mold can affect both your health and the structural integrity of your home if left unaddressed. The very first thing to do is figure out why it's there.

Mold needs moisture to grow, so identifying and stopping the water source, whether it's a leaky pipe, a humid room, or a roof issue, is step one. Just cleaning visible mold without fixing the leak is a common mistake, and the mold will almost certainly return. After you've stopped the water, then you can think about removing the mold.

For smaller spots, you might be able to handle it yourself with proper safety gear. But honestly, if the mold is widespread, hidden, or keeps coming back, that’s when you absolutely need professionals involved. They can find hidden moisture sources with specialized equipment and ensure the contamination is completely removed and doesn't just reappear.
