Steps to Take When Your Kitchen Has Had a Fire

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Web Pages Q&A

When a kitchen fire happens, it can feel overwhelming, but there's a clear path to getting your space back. First, make sure everyone is safe and the fire is completely extinguished. Your immediate safety is always the priority.

Step 1: Secure the Area. Once the fire department has cleared your home, take photos of the damage for insurance purposes. You'll also want to make sure any utilities like gas or electricity to the affected area are shut off if there’s any potential hazard.

Step 2: Prevent Further Damage. Smoke and soot residues are corrosive. Don't touch anything unnecessarily as oils from your hands can set stains.

Avoid using standard household cleaners; they can spread soot and embed it further. If you try to run your HVAC system, you could spread smoke odor and soot throughout your entire home. Step 3: Call a Professional Restoration Company.
