My Garage Caught Fire, Now What?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Web Pages Q&A

Discovering your garage has been impacted by fire can be really unsettling, especially considering what garages often hold – vehicles, tools, storage. The good news is, there's a straightforward process to get things sorted and minimize further damage. It’s crucial to act quickly and wisely.

First things first: safety. Make sure the fire is completely out and the fire department has given you the go-ahead to re-enter. Never risk your safety to salvage items.

Once it's safe, you'll need to ventilate the garage as much as possible to help air out smoke and fumes. Open any doors or windows. Next, carefully assess the extent of the damage.

Is it just a small corner, or has the fire spread significantly? Look for structural damage, burnt contents, and areas coated in soot. Be really careful around any potentially compromised structures or electrical wiring.

It's often a good idea to shut off power to the garage if it's safe to do so. Finally, and this is a big one, don't underestimate the persistent nature of smoke and soot. These aren't just cosmetic issues; they can cause long-term corrosion and health problems.
