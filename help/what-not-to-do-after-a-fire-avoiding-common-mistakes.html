What Not to Do After a Fire: Avoiding Common Mistakes

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Web Pages Q&A

Experiencing a fire in your home or business is incredibly stressful, and it's easy to make mistakes in the immediate aftermath. One of the biggest errors people make is trying to clean up soot and smoke damage themselves without proper knowledge or equipment. You might think a quick scrub will do the trick, but often, this just smears the soot, making the damage worse and harder to remove later.

Another common mistake is turning on all your appliances or HVAC system right away. Soot and smoke can enter these systems and spread contamination throughout your property, even to areas not directly affected by the fire. You should also avoid throwing away fire-damaged items too quickly.

Some items, even seemingly ruined ones, can often be restored by professionals. It’s important to document everything for insurance purposes before discarding anything. Lastly, don't delay calling in professional help.

The longer smoke and soot sit, the more deeply they penetrate surfaces, leading to permanent staining and odors. Acting fast can make a huge difference in the restoration process.
