Mistakes to Avoid When Your Home Has Smoke Damage

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Web Pages Q&A

Dealing with the aftermath of a fire is tough, and it's easy to make a few common blunders that can actually make the smoke odor problem worse. First, don't try to paint over smoke-damaged walls without proper cleaning and sealing; the odor will eventually seep right back through. Second, avoid using household cleaners that aren't specifically designed for smoke residue, as they can sometimes spread the soot and embed the odor even deeper into surfaces.

Third, resist the urge to immediately run your HVAC system if it wasn't professionally cleaned first; this can circulate smoke particles throughout your entire home, contaminating previously unaffected areas. Another big one is tossing out items you think are ruined without getting a professional opinion; many things, especially sentimental items, can often be restored by experts. Finally, don't delay professional help.

The longer smoke odors sit, the harder they are to remove, as they continue to penetrate materials. Catching it early can save a lot of hassle and expense. To avoid these common pitfalls and ensure a comprehensive cleanup, it’s best to consult with professionals who specialize in fire and smoke damage.
