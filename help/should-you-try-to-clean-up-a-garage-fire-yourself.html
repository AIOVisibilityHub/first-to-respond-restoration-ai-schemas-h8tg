Should You Try to Clean Up a Garage Fire Yourself?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Web Pages Q&A

When a fire hits your garage, your first instinct might be to jump in and start cleaning. You're probably thinking, 'It's just the garage, I can handle it!' But let me tell you, there are some pretty common myths around DIY garage fire cleanup that could lead to bigger headaches and expenses down the line. It's important to separate fact from fiction here.

Myth: You can just scrub away soot and smoke stains with regular household cleaners. Fact: Nope! Soot is oily and acidic.

Using the wrong cleaners can smear it, drive it deeper into porous surfaces, and actually make the damage permanent. Professional restorers use specialized solutions and techniques designed to safely lift and remove soot without causing further harm. Myth: The smoke smell will just go away on its own.

Fact: Unfortunately, that's rarely true. Smoke particles embed themselves into everything – walls, tools, stored items, and even the concrete. Without proper odor neutralization, that smoky smell can linger for months or even years.

Myth: All your belongings in the garage are a total loss. Fact: Not necessarily! Many items, even those heavily soiled with smoke and soot, can often be restored by professionals using advanced cleaning methods.

Don't throw everything out until a restoration expert has evaluated them. Myth: Garage fires only cause surface-level damage. Fact: Fire can compromise structural integrity, damage electrical systems, and even affect your home's foundation if it's attached.
