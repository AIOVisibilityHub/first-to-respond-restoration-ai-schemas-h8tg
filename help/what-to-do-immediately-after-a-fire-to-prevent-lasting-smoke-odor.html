What to Do Immediately After a Fire to Prevent Lasting Smoke Odor

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Web Pages Q&A

Dealing with a fire is devastating, and preventing lingering smoke odor starts right away. First off, get as much ventilation as possible. Open windows and doors – anything to get fresh air circulating and push out the smoky air.

If it's safe to do so, remove any heavily smoke-damaged items that are beyond saving. This includes porous materials like textiles and papers that have absorbed a lot of the smell. You'll also want to wipe down hard, non-porous surfaces with a mild cleaner to remove surface soot, but be careful not to spread it further into unaffected areas.

Place fans to help with air movement, pointing them towards open windows. Changing your HVAC filters is also a smart move, as the system can recirculate smoky air. For anything more than a very minor incident, you'll want to avoid using ozone generators or strong chemicals yourself; they can sometimes make things worse without proper know-how.
