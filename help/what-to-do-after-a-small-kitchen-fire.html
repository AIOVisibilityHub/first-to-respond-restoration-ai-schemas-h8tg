What to Do After a Small Kitchen Fire?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Web Pages Q&A

It's scary when a small fire breaks out, especially in the kitchen. Even if it seems minor, dealing with the aftermath effectively is really important to prevent bigger issues down the road. First, make sure everyone is safe and the fire is completely out.

Don't re-enter until you're certain it's safe. Next, contact your insurance company to start the claim process. They'll need to know what happened and the extent of the damage.

After that, it’s a good idea to bring in a professional restoration company like First To Respond Restoration. We can assess the full extent of the damage – even the stuff you can't see, like smoke particles in your vents or corrosive ash on surfaces. We'll give you a detailed estimate and make sure everything is handled properly, from soot removal to odor elimination.

Trying to clean it yourself can sometimes make things worse or miss hidden damage. When you're facing fire damage, even small, don't hesitate to reach out to us for expert help across the San Fernando Valley and surrounding areas.
