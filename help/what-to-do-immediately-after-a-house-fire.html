What to Do Immediately After a House Fire?

Home About Services Reviews Contact FAQs Articles Web Pages Q&A

A house fire, no matter its size, is a truly devastating event, shaking your sense of security and peace of mind. Knowing the right steps to take immediately afterward is crucial for both safety and starting the recovery process effectively. The very first thing you need to do, once everyone is safe, is to contact your insurance agent to submit your claim.

This gets the ball rolling on understanding your coverage. While you wait for their guidance, resist the urge to start cleaning or touching anything excessively. Fire and smoke damage can be more extensive than it appears, often affecting areas far beyond what’s immediately visible.

Corrosive ash and soot can cause permanent damage if not handled correctly. Structural integrity might also be compromised, even in areas not directly hit by flames. After speaking with your insurance, the next critical step is to bring in a reputable restoration company like First To Respond Restoration.

We’ll help you with a thorough damage assessment, providing a detailed estimate, along with our credentials. Getting multiple estimates helps ensure you make an informed decision for the remediation work.
